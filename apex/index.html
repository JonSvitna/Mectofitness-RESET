The Execution Standard™

Five principles that define how this program operates.

These are not motivational concepts. They are operating conditions.

  • Consistency Is the Method Isolated effort produces isolated results. Repeated, deliberate execution across 90 days is the mechanism — not willpower, not inspiration.
  • Data Over Feeling Progress is not assessed by how you feel. It is measured, documented, and evaluated against defined benchmarks on a fixed schedule.
  • Protocol Precedes Preference Personal preferences are acknowledged at intake. After that, the protocol governs. Adjustments are data-driven, not comfort-driven.
  • Accountability Is Structural Accountability is not optional encouragement — it is built into the architecture of the program. Non-compliance is visible and addressed.
  • Completion Is the Outcome The program ends when 90 days are completed — not when progress slows, not when discomfort rises. Completion is the standard.
